# Expected Output — Project Risk

## Executive answer

The highest-priority project risks are P002, P001, P005 and P007. P003 also requires attention because a critical supplier dependency is threatening schedule and margin.

The most important distinction is that not all red projects are failing for the same reason. Some are driven by scope governance, some by supplier dependency, some by customer changes and some by weak contract economics.

## Priority ranking

### 1. P002 — Orion EV Component Cell

**Forecast margin: 19% vs 33% planned**  
**Forecast delay: approximately 5 weeks**

P002 is the most severe combined risk.

Evidence includes:

- €88k unbilled scope change,
- €52k overtime,
- €31k rework,
- scope expansion accepted before commercial approval,
- forecast completion pushed from 15 November to 20 December,
- additional engineering work still commercially unresolved.

Recommended action: immediate joint Operations + Commercial + Finance recovery review focused on scope monetization, remaining cost-to-complete and delivery commitment.

### 2. P001 — Helios Line 7 Automation

**Forecast margin: 24% vs 32% planned**  
**Forecast delay: 21 days**

Main drivers are material inflation, overtime and unbilled change requests.

The latest project-control event reduced forecast margin from 27% to 24%, indicating deterioration is still active rather than historical.

Recommended action: resolve change-order economics and validate the latest remaining-material and overtime forecast.

### 3. P005 — NovaGlass Furnace Controls

**Forecast margin: 21% vs 29% planned**  
**Forecast delay: 23 days**

The project combines:

- late customer design changes,
- €27k unbilled scope,
- €29k overtime,
- €18k rework,
- a customer escalation about delivery slippage,
- and an outstanding invoice balance being held while the delivery complaint remains open.

Recommended action: treat this as a combined delivery-recovery, commercial-scope and customer-trust problem.

### 4. P007 — Delta Water SCADA Retrofit

**Forecast margin: 11% vs 18% planned**  
**Forecast delay: 22 days**

Although smaller than P001 or P002, this contract is economically weak and still deteriorating.

Repeated rework, overtime, unbilled change work and customer approval delays are compounded by poor customer payment behavior.

Recommended action: determine whether the correct response is recovery, repricing of remaining changes, or strict containment of further unbilled effort.

### 5. P003 — Lusitania Pharma Packaging Upgrade

**Forecast margin: 29% vs 35% planned**  
**Forecast delay: 26 days**

The largest identified driver is external supplier S003.

S003 is a critical machine-vision dependency with 68% on-time delivery, 19 average delay days and 11% year-on-year price inflation. A recent event added another nine-day delay.

Recommended action: executive supplier escalation plus evaluation of contingency sourcing where technically viable.

## Lower-risk comparators

P004, P006 and P008 currently show substantially healthier margin and execution characteristics and should not consume the same level of executive attention.

## Recommended executive actions

1. Put P002 into immediate cross-functional recovery governance.
2. Close or price pending change orders on P001 and P005.
3. Contain further unbilled effort on P007.
4. Escalate S003 dependency for P003 and examine alternatives.
5. Require updated cost-to-complete and schedule confidence for every high-risk project.

## Confidence

**High** on project economics, forecast dates and recorded operational signals.

**Medium** on final recovery outcomes because resource availability, contractual detail and full supplier alternatives are not represented in the public dataset.

## Actions requiring approval

Resource reallocation, contractual changes, supplier replacement, incremental spend and revised external delivery commitments require human authorization.
