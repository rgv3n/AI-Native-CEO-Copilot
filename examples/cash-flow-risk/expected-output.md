# Expected Output — Cash-Flow Risk

## Executive answer

Cash is being trapped primarily in receivables linked to a small number of customers, with several cases where collection risk is connected to unresolved commercial or delivery issues rather than simple payment timing.

The most material exposures are Orion Components, Delta Water, Helios Automotive and NovaGlass.

## Priority findings

### 1. Orion Components — highest material exposure

I003 and I004 represent **€500k unpaid**. I003 is materially overdue and I004 is also overdue. The records connect the delay to scope reconciliation and revised commercial documentation.

This is both a collections issue and a project-commercial governance issue.

### 2. Delta Water — highest payment-behavior risk

I009 and I010 represent **€215k overdue**. The latest event states that another promised payment date was missed and expected collection was moved later.

This should be treated as elevated collection risk, but the dataset does not justify declaring default.

### 3. Helios Automotive — disputed cash conversion

I001 has **€100k outstanding** after a partial payment. The remaining amount is connected to a change-order discussion.

P001 also contains unbilled scope changes, showing a wider scope-to-cash problem.

### 4. NovaGlass — delivery issue affecting collection

I007 has **€80k outstanding** and its notes state the balance is being held while a delivery complaint remains open.

The project itself is delayed and has unbilled scope changes, so the collection issue should not be treated independently from execution.

## Company-level signal

Cash has fallen from **€1.18M in April to €680k in September**, while accounts receivable increased from **€980k to €1.51M**.

The trend indicates weakening cash conversion despite revenue growth.

## Recommended executive actions

1. Put Orion's €500k exposure into a joint Finance + Commercial + Operations resolution process.
2. Escalate Delta Water collections and review credit exposure before taking additional commercial risk.
3. Resolve change-order approval and billing blockers on Helios and other active projects.
4. Tie NovaGlass collections recovery to the delivery-recovery plan rather than treating it as a standalone finance action.
5. Review all unbilled scope changes weekly until working-capital pressure normalizes.

## Confidence

**High** on invoice balances, overdue status and cash/AR trend.

**Medium-high** on the relationship between project/commercial issues and delayed collections because those links are supported by invoice notes and event records but not by a full customer communication history.

## Actions requiring approval

The Copilot may prepare collection plans and draft communications, but customer escalation, changes to payment terms, credit holds or accounting actions require explicit human authorization.
