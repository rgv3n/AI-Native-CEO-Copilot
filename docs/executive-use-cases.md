# Executive Use Cases

The goal of CEO Copilot is not to answer generic questions. It is to help executives investigate business situations using trusted company data.

## 1. Margin deterioration

**Executive question**

> Why did our margin fall this month?

**Potential data sources**

- revenue by project or customer,
- direct costs,
- labor costs,
- procurement changes,
- discounts,
- scope changes,
- delivery delays.

**Expected output**

- primary drivers,
- quantified contribution where possible,
- affected projects or clients,
- source references,
- confidence level,
- recommended investigation or action.

## 2. Cash getting trapped

**Executive question**

> Where is cash getting trapped?

**Potential data sources**

- accounts receivable,
- accounts payable,
- invoice aging,
- customer payment behavior,
- project milestones,
- committed expenses.

**Expected output**

- major cash bottlenecks,
- overdue concentration,
- customers or projects creating exposure,
- near-term liquidity risks,
- suggested priorities for intervention.

## 3. Sales pipeline prioritization

**Executive question**

> Which opportunities deserve executive attention this week?

**Potential data sources**

- CRM pipeline,
- deal size,
- stage,
- probability,
- recent activity,
- historical win rates,
- account importance.

**Expected output**

- ranked opportunities,
- reasons for ranking,
- risk signals,
- missing actions,
- recommended next moves.

## 4. Project risk

**Executive question**

> Which projects are likely to miss margin or delivery targets?

**Potential data sources**

- project plans,
- actual vs planned costs,
- delivery milestones,
- material availability,
- subcontractor status,
- scope changes.

**Expected output**

- project risk ranking,
- likely root causes,
- estimated business impact,
- evidence used,
- recommended review actions.

## 5. Executive attention system

**Executive question**

> What deserves my attention today?

This is the synthesis use case.

The system should compare signals across finance, sales and operations, then surface only the items that cross defined thresholds for urgency, economic impact or strategic relevance.

The objective is not to create more notifications. It is to compress company complexity into a small number of decision-relevant signals.
